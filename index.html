About›

Dennis Pitallano — known in certain corners of the internet as Oragon — is a full-stack software developer based in the Philippines, spending his career turning business requirements into running software and, occasionally, the reverse.

Born of the Bicol Region, he has worked across web, mobile, and blockchain, with a career-long preference for correctness over cleverness and a willingness to read the stack trace in full.

His work is characterised by an unfashionable belief that a good commit message is worth two bad meetings, and that naming things well is the hardest and most important part of the craft.
